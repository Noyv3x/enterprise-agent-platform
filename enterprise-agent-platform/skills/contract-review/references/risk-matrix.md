# Risk matrix

**Score = Severity (1-5) × Likelihood (1-5).** Adjust the money bands to the company's size and the contract value; state the bands used.

| Severity | Meaning (exposure relative to contract/deal value) |
|---|---|
| 1 Negligible | handled in normal operations |
| 2 Low | < 1%, minor disruption |
| 3 Moderate | 1-5%, noticeable disruption |
| 4 High | 5-25%, significant disruption, likely regulatory attention |
| 5 Critical | > 25%, core business, severe reputational or personal liability for 法定代表人/高管 |

| Likelihood | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 Remote | would need exceptional circumstances |
| 2 Unlikely | specific trigger needed |
| 3 Possible | triggers foreseeable |
| 4 Likely | common in similar situations |
| 5 Almost certain | already present or imminent |

| Score | Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | 🟢 low | accept, record, normal approval |
| 5-9 | 🟡 medium | negotiate, owner + monitoring |
| 10-15 | 🟠 high | 法务负责人 review, mitigation plan before signing |
| 16-25 | 🔴 critical | do not sign without 总法律顾问/外部律师 and 业务负责人 sign-off |

Escalate regardless of score: uncapped liability; exclusivity/non-compete beyond the business need; assignment of pre-existing IP; personal-information processing or export without a lawful basis; terms conflicting with regulation or with other contracts; unusual signing/seal arrangements; counterparty insolvency signals.

Memo fields: risk description, context, severity+reason, likelihood+reason, contributing and mitigating factors, mitigation options (cost/effect), recommendation (accept / mitigate / negotiate / escalate / reject), residual risk, review date.
