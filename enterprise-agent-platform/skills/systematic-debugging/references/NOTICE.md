## What was changed

- Rewritten as a compact SKILL.md; the four-phase method, one-hypothesis rule and three-failures rule are retained.
- `references/root-cause-tracing.md`, `defense-in-depth.md` and `condition-based-waiting.md` are condensed rewrites of the upstream files of the same names (examples and the bisection script were not copied; the bisection is described without touching the user's tree).
- Added for this platform: preservation of user work, no destructive reproduction, and parent-owned verification when subagents help. Upstream cross-skill references and its "human partner" framing were removed.
